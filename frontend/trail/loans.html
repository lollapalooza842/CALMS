    [05] Questions

    Asked often

    [01]What do I need to apply?

    A government ID and proof of income for every loan. A salary loan also asks for pay stubs and a certificate of employment; an SME loan asks for your DTI or SEC registration, business permit and financial statements.

    [02]How long until I get the money?

    It depends on how complete your papers are. You can see where your loan is at any time on the Track page, and we text you at every step.

    [03]What is a counter-offer?

    If we can't lend the full amount or term you asked for, we may offer a different one. You decide: take it, or turn it down at no cost.

    [04]How do I pay?

    Cash at the branch, bank deposit, GCash, salary deduction (salary loans) or cheque. Every payment gets a receipt in My GR8.

    [05]What if I pay late?

    A late payment adds 3% of the late amount for each month or part of a month it stays unpaid. Pay what's due and the penalty stops growing.

    [06]Can I have two loans at once?

    You can hold different kinds, but only one application per kind of loan can be open at a time.
