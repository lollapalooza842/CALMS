Setting it up

Three steps

1

Download and run

Open the downloaded file. If Windows asks whether to allow it, choose More info, then Run anyway.

2

Pick where it goes

Keep the suggested folder or choose your own. A CALMS shortcut lands on your desktop.

3

Sign in

Open CALMS and sign in as you would in the browser. Your queue is waiting.

Which one?

App or browser

Both open the same CALMS with the same account. Use whichever suits your desk.
