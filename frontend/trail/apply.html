Apply for a loan

Five short steps, about ten minutes. Have a photo of your ID ready.

How it works

  1. You apply — Fill in the form below. About ten minutes; have a photo of your ID ready.
  2. We assess — A loan processor reviews your papers and our system checks your credit report from FinScore, the BSP-accredited bureau. This usually takes one working day.
  3. We decide — We send you the offer: the approved amount, term, and monthly payment. You have time to review it before signing anything.
  4. Funds released — Once signed, we release the money. Salary and personal loans typically via bank transfer; SME loans are coordinated with your preferred channel.

What is FinScore?

FinScore is the Philippines' BSP-supervised credit bureau. When you apply, we send a short inquiry — with your consent — and FinScore returns a score (300–850) and your credit history. A higher score means a longer, cleaner track record. Your score is one of several things we look at; a low score does not automatically mean a refusal.

Requirements & eligibility

To borrow from GR8 you must be at least 18 years old and a Philippine resident. Beyond that, each loan type has its own list:

Salary loan · up to ₱200,000
Government ID · Pay stubs · Certificate of employment · Proof of billing · Bank statement
Personal loan · up to ₱150,000
Government ID · Proof of income · Proof of billing
SME loan · up to ₱1,000,000
Government ID · DTI or SEC registration · Financial statements · Proof of address · Business permit · Bank statements

How we assess

Our system checks three things automatically. First, your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) — how much of your monthly income goes to existing loan payments, including the one you're applying for. We look for DTI below 50%. Second, your FinScore from the credit bureau. Third, for loans with collateral, the loan-to-value ratio (LTV). A loan processor then reviews the full picture and makes the call. No single number is a hard disqualifier; we read the whole story.
